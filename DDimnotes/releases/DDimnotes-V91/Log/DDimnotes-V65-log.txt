DDimnotes V65 - depth dimension fix

1. Side-view depth dimensions now use the segment emitted by the side-view planner.
   - Point 1: depth bottom inside the side view.
   - Point 2: hole opening edge on the side view.

2. Depth holes are no longer dimensioned by recalculating start/end in the dimension step.
   - Planner records SideDepthDimSegment while generating side-view geometry.
   - Dimension step only consumes these recorded points.

3. Same-center depth holes are not stacked for the depth segment start anymore.
   - Every hole with Depth != 0 starts from its side-view opening edge and ends at its own depth bottom.

4. PickPoint movement is preserved.
   - Recorded depth-dim segment points are shifted by the side-view move vector before dimensions are created.
