DDimnotes V24
- Base: V23.
- Reworked settings dialog layout to avoid clipped rows and cramped groups.
- Mode combo now shows user-friendly Vietnamese names only; D/B/A remain internal keywords.
- Angle combo remains a simple single-choice list.
- Split spacing into two independent settings:
  + Side-view spacing scale: multiplier of DIM text height.
  + Notes spacing scale: multiplier of the actual main-to-side-view distance.
- Side-view gap now uses SideViewDistanceScale * DIMTXT instead of fixed 8 * DIMTXT.
- Version bumped to 1.0.24.0.
