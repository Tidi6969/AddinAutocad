DDimnotes V41

- Updated assembly version to 1.0.41.0.
- Added dedicated set_screw handling for XData codes such as set_screwBSPR.
- set_screw now uses the MSW section in ti.dat.
- For set_screw, the core/tap drill diameter from MSW is always drawn as a through hole over the full plate thickness.
- The nominal M diameter follows the signed depth, including negative depth from the back face.
- The end of the nominal-depth thread keeps the angled/peak thread geometry, not a flat step face.
- Added BSPR tag before SPR so Notes do not classify set_screwBSPR as Spring.
